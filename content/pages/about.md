Jean Cochrane is a programmer who solves data problems to help make government
faster, fairer, and more accessible. She likes bringing modern data tools to
bear on thorny issues of data integrity and governance. She lives and works
in Chicago, IL.

Jean is currently a senior data scientist at the [Cook County Assessor's
Office](https://www.cookcountyassessoril.gov/), where she is a technical leader
working to make property tax assessments fairer and more transparent through
data science and data engineering. Previously, she was a senior backend engineer
at [Sendwave](https://www.sendwave.com/), where she built infrastructure to help
migrants send money to family and friends. Further back, Jean built civic apps
with [DataMade](https://datamade.us), helped manage cloud infrastructure for
[Azavea](https://www.azavea.com/), was a reporting fellow at [City
Bureau](https://www.citybureau.org/), and made comics and graphics for the
[South Side Weekly](https://southsideweekly.com/). She attended the [Recurse
Center](https://recurse.com) in Fall 2018 and she encourages you to do so, too.

Off the clock, Jean leads the indie pop band [Hard
Femme](https://hardfemme.bandcamp.com/), volunteers on her ward's zoning
advisory council, and is the captain of her queer women's softball team.
She is often [on her bike](https://hardfemme.bandcamp.com/track/on-my-bike).
